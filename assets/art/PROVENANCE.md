### World map prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: landscape 2D strategy campaign map background, 16:9. Primary request: a beautiful hand-painted illustrated medieval border region viewed from nearly overhead, with no horizon. A narrow blue-green coastline occupies the far left 10 percent, rich olive/emerald meadows fill the center, soft wooded hills fill the top and right edges. A single pale ochre road runs horizontally across the middle at 52 percent image height from the left coast toward the right forest. Tiny slate-roof hamlets are placed along that road near 12 percent and 53 percent image width. Small fields, a mill, meadow flowers, pale rocks and patches of trees give it a detailed living storybook feel. Keep the central road and meadows spacious enough for small movable party icons. Warm upper-left sunlight, soft painted brush textures, elegant selective ink edges, blue-green shadows, restrained parchment-gold highlights; visually match a sophisticated hand-painted medieval tactical RPG. Constraints: original game asset, purely 2D illustration, continuous terrain, no hexagons, no grid, no tile borders, no labels, no lettering, no UI, no people, no units, no icon markers, no watermark.

## Final prompt set

### battlefield

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: painted 2D tactical game battlefield background, landscape 16:9, for The History of Berania. Primary request: beautiful hand-painted medieval countryside with a broad open grassy meadow taking up the central 80 percent of the image, viewed at a fixed elevated three-quarter angle looking downward, no horizon or sky. A winding pale ochre dirt road crosses from lower-left toward upper-right. A tiny slate-roof stone village and a water mill occupy only the far upper-left border; distant wooded ridges and a few pale boulders frame the upper and right borders. Warm morning sunlight from upper-left, olive and emerald foliage, blue-green shadows, soft brush textures, restrained fine ink accents, sophisticated illustrated storybook aesthetic, detailed grass at edges but uncluttered flat playable center. Composition: central meadow must have ample clear empty room for six separate troop formations, buildings and thick trees kept at perimeter. A few small flat decorative stones are fine. Constraints: original game art, all illustration is 2D, no characters, no units, no robots, no hexes, no grid, no labels, no text, no UI, no watermark, no buildings or large obstacles in central meadow.

### portrait

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original transparent-background 2D portrait for a medieval industrial strategy RPG. Primary request: a single adult female border captain, waist-up, three-quarter view facing slightly right, calm resolute expression, shoulder-length dark brown hair partially tied back, warm olive skin, slate-blue cloak over practical weathered steel brigandine with restrained brass fittings, small ivory embroidered heraldic sun on shoulder, one gloved hand resting at belt, unhelmeted. Style: beautifully hand-painted storybook realism, expressive face, elegant selective ink lines, textured brush shading, warm upper-left light and cool navy shadows; grounded medieval clothing, no futuristic suit. Composition: entire hair, shoulders and hands comfortably inside the frame; cropped at lower hips; isolated on genuine transparent background. Constraints: no text, no lettering, no frame, no scenery, no logos, no watermark, original character.
