# Berania — Detailed 2D Art Direction

**Current user decision:** November 3, 2026. Return to the good detailed artwork and implement main game functions first.

## Active presentation

- Painted/inked whole-character 2D art, with ivory/navy/gold Oathkeeper and painted medieval-magitech scenery.
- Existing source: `assets/art/mechs/oathkeeper/fullbody/v004/oathkeeper-illustrated-actions.png` and its manifest. Pixel-v001 is preserved offline and excluded from active delivery.
- Fixed complete designs and permanent weapons. Oathkeeper always carries its sword and shield; no modular weapons, detached parts or visual assembly.

- Keep existing idle, walking, attack, guard and hit presentation. The earlier walk/attack problems are not newly approved or claimed solved by restoring the artwork.
- Existing portraits, painted world/battle backgrounds and temporary supporting unit figures remain usable while the main systems are built.

## Work priority

Implement quests, headquarters, party/pilots, economy, diplomacy, conflict and the complete story routes in [the main plan](initial-plan.md). Do not delay them with further animation generation, style comparisons or review loops.

After the main functions exist, detailed 2D art may expand to supported fixed-design units without changing their gameplay rules. Retain low-knee normal walking, clear attack timing and complete-character frames as the desired motion direction. Do not claim a pose sheet or increased frame count establishes smooth animation.

## Excluded work

No 3D models, rigs, renders, rotation sets, segmented bodies, custom weapons, pixel-style experiments, art QA sessions, capture/comparison exercises or performance gates. No paid art tools or services. Existing source/history stays preserved outside active production.

## Integration

Use the existing Compatibility renderer, fixed viewpoint and landscape controls. Keep character scale, frame anchors, canonical ready/guard returns, pause/speed/reduced motion and the existing authoritative timing contracts. Art selection never applies damage, advances strategic time or changes rewards.

Keep the approved concept identities for Oathkeeper and the future Cinderjack/Kestrel/Bastion concepts; this is not approval of unseen final animation or a reason to generate the full roster before the main game works.
